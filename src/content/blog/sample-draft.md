## Start with the work

Before comparing platforms, describe the part of the day that is getting in the way. Who is affected? What are they trying to do? What would a useful improvement look like?

The answer might be a new subscription, a better connection between existing tools, a custom application, or a simpler way to use what is already there.

## Questions worth asking

- What is working well enough to keep?
- Where is information being entered more than once?
- Who will look after the solution when the initial work is done?
